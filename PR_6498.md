Closes #6498

## Summary

Discord report: **Portent of Calamity** did not let you keep selected revealed cards — they went to the graveyard.

Oracle: `Reveal the top X cards … For each card type, you may exile a card of that type from among them. Put the rest into your graveyard. …`

## Root cause

1. **"Put the rest into your graveyard"** was parsed as `ChangeZoneAll { Exile → Graveyard, TrackedSet }`. After `ForEachCategory` exile, the tracked set is the *exiled* picks — so selecting a card immediately dumped it to the graveyard.
2. Dig→`RevealTop` demotion collapsed non-fixed counts (X) to `1`.

## Changes

- Keep dynamic-count reveal Digs as reveal-only peeks (`keep_count: 0`) instead of collapsing X to `RevealTop { count: 1 }`.
- After `ForEachCategory` exile, "put the rest" emits `ChangeZoneAll { Library → Graveyard, LastRevealed }` so only non-exiled revealed cards go to the graveyard.
- Publish `CardsRevealed` from reveal-only Digs into the chain tracked-set path; `ForEachCategory` pool falls back to `last_revealed_ids`.
